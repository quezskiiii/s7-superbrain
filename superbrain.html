Safety Rules for Auto-Copy SAFE MODE
Auto-Copy: Buys practice copies by itself when a coin passes enough checks. For wallet copies, two different wallets you follow must buy the same coin within 60 seconds before Auto-Copy opens one.
Mirror-Sell: Closes your practice copy the moment the wallet you copied sells.
Drawdown floor: Auto-Copy pauses if your practice money drops to $3.50 or below (this is a floor, not a daily limit — it does not reset each day).
Initials out at 2x: When a practice trade doubles, the app automatically sells half — that puts your starting money back into Practice Money, and the other half keeps riding as house money.
Profit Ladder: On the way up, each practice trade sells a piece at your Step 1 multiple and another piece at your Step 2 multiple (shipped as 25% at 1.30x and 25% at 1.50x — change them in the settings above). Each step fires once, at the real price of the tick that reaches it, and the money goes back to Practice Money. What is left is the runner, and it stays under the normal rules — initials-out at 2x on what remains, then bag-lock, trailing stop, stop-loss, time exit, and the 45-minute max hold. Practice trades only — the ladder never touches a real position.
Bag-lock: After initials are out, the half still riding is risk-free house money, so if it falls back to 1.1x or below the app closes it right away to lock in the win — that 1.1x floor replaces your normal stop-loss for that remainder only, and the trailing stop still protects it above that floor.
Trailing stop: Once a practice trade is up 50% or more, if it then falls 25% down from its highest point, the app closes the rest so a winner does not turn into a loser.
Time exit: If a practice trade has been open more than 12 minutes and is still up less than 15%, the app closes it because it went nowhere.
Losing wallets: If practice copies from the same followed wallet lose 4 times in a row, the app stops following that wallet automatically.
God Mode: One tap turns on the strict 7 of 8 sniper gate, Auto-Copy, Mirror-Sell, and starts the live stream. Turning it off puts the gate back to 6 of 8 — Auto-Copy and Mirror-Sell stay wherever you set them yourself.
God Mode profit sweep: While God Mode is on, half of every practice profit goes into the Safe Vault where Auto-Copy cannot spend it, and the other half plus your starting money go back to Practice Money.
God Mode moonbag: While God Mode is on, after initials-out the half that keeps riding aims for 4.0x instead of your normal take-profit.
God Mode and real money: God Mode never spends real money by itself. Auto-Copy and Mirror-Sell stay practice-only even in God Mode — real trades are still only your tap on a blue button plus your signature in your wallet app.
